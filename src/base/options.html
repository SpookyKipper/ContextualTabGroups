Contextual Tab Groups Options

Welcome to Contextual Tab Groups

This extension groups your tabs for you.

Quick start: your first group

  1. Open the Groups tab and click “New group”.
  2. Pick a name and a color, e.g. “Dev”, yellow.
  3. Add a domain under it, e.g. github.com.
  4. Open github.com: the tab moves into the yellow “Dev” group by itself.

Also good to know

  • Open a link in a new tab and both tabs are grouped automatically.
  • Alt+T opens a new tab inside the current tab's group.
  • The Behavior tab sets automatic group names and disbanding of single-tab groups.

Everything stays on your device; the extension sends no data anywhere.

Modify Open in current (new) Tab Group hotkey
Default: Alt+T

Firefox's menu bar may interfere with the default Alt+T shortcut. Please either:
1. Change to another shortcut key yourself
2. Go to about:config and set ui.key.menuAccessKey to 0
